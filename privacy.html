Privacy notice

Last updated 14 November 2026.

PSM INFINITY is a private concierge service run by Sunandan Mitra in Kolkata, India. This notice explains what happens to the details you send us. It is short because we collect very little.

What we collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form: your name, email address, country, contact number, your message, and your consent to us replying. Nothing else is recorded.

What we do not collect

This website sets no advertising cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. There is no account to create and no profile built about you.

How your enquiry travels

The form does not post to a database on this server. It composes a message and opens WhatsApp so that you can send it yourself, which means the message is delivered through WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) under their own privacy terms. If you would rather not use WhatsApp, write to us by email or telephone instead, using the details on the homepage.

Why we hold it

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if you engage us, to carry out the request.
  • To keep a record of instructions you have given us.
  • Nothing else. We do not sell, rent or trade your details.

Who sees it

Your details are seen by Sunandan Mitra and, where a request requires it, by the supplier or venue needed to fulfil it, and only to the extent required. Client names, itineraries and purchases are never published or used as references without your consent.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted within twelve months. Records relating to an engagement are kept for as long as the engagement lasts and for the period Indian tax and company law requires afterwards.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete it. Write to psminfinity@gmail.com and we will respond within thirty days.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS. Access to correspondence is limited to the principal. No system is perfect, so please do not send sensitive documents through the enquiry form.
